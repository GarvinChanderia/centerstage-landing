Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, sizing, and shipping.

What makes Center Stage fabrics different?

Center Stage uses natural-origin, bio-derived fibers that are soft, breathable, and gentle on the skin. Our fabrics are specifically chosen for the Indian climate — naturally antibacterial, pre-shrunk, and engineered for all-day comfort.

Is Center Stage clothing suitable for the office?

Yes. Center Stage is designed for smart casual everyday wear — workdays, client meetings, and team outings. Each piece transitions effortlessly from professional environments to social weekend plans.

Does Center Stage offer cash on delivery?

Yes, Center Stage offers cash on delivery across India.

What is Center Stage's return policy?

Center Stage offers a 15-day return policy on all purchases. You can initiate a return by contacting our team at sm@centerstage.store or via WhatsApp.

Where does Center Stage ship and how do I track my order?

Center Stage is based in India and ships nationwide. You can track your order at delhivery.com/tracking.
